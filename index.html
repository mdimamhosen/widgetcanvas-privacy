Privacy Policy — Widget Canvas

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Widget Canvas (“the app”) is an Android home-screen widget creator published by Md Imam Hosen.

What we collect

The app does not require an account. Widget designs, notes, habits, selected photos, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a Widget Canvas backend that receives that content.

Permissions and on-device features

  • Calendar (optional): with your permission, the app may read event title and time to show the next event on a widget. It does not modify your calendar.
  • Photos (optional): you choose images through the system picker; a private local copy may be kept for the widget.
  • Location (optional): used only when you request weather for a place you select. Coordinates are sent to MET Norway for forecast data.
  • Bluetooth / network state (optional): used only for status-style widgets you add.

Network use

When online, the app may contact:

  • MET Norway — weather forecasts for a city or coordinates you choose (standard request metadata such as IP may be logged by that service).
  • Frankfurter / ECB open data — currency rates for the currency widget.
  • Google Play Billing — purchases and restore of Widget Canvas Pro.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — ads in the creator app for free users (banner, native, optional rewarded). Ads are never shown inside home-screen widgets. In the EEA/UK, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is shown before ads are requested when required.

Advertising

If you are not a Pro subscriber/lifetime purchaser and advertising is allowed after consent/choice where required, Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers (including Advertising ID), approximate device data, and ad interaction data under Google’s policies. Pro removes ads in the creator app. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android settings.

Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob

Purchases

One-time and subscription purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience (13+ on Play). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are not directed to children.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete a widget, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. For privacy questions or applicable access/deletion requests, contact the email below.
